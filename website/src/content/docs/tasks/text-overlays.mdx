**Overlays** in the context menu puts the same text overlays on many cameras: date and time, the camera name, your own
text. It has one entry per action: **Add overlays**, **Replace overlays**, **Remove matching overlays** and, last,
**Remove all overlays**.

<Image src={shot} alt="The Add overlays dialog with date and time bottom left and the camera name top right" class="screen" />

## Run it

<Steps>

1. Select the cameras, right-click and choose **Overlays** and the action:
   - **Add overlays**: the overlays go next to those the camera has.
   - **Replace overlays**: the camera keeps exactly this list.
   - **Remove matching overlays**: removes the overlays with the same text.
   - **Remove all overlays**: removes every text overlay after you confirm. No list is needed.
2. Type the **Text** of each overlay. The button next to it adds a value at the end of the text: date, time, day of the week,
   date and time, camera name, IP address, MAC address, frame rate, bitrate or dynamic text 1 to 16.
3. Choose **Position**, **Size**, **Text colour**, **Background** and **Outline**. An empty **Size** keeps the camera's
   default. **Add overlay** adds another one, up to 8.
4. Click **Add overlays**, **Replace overlays** or **Remove overlays**.

</Steps>

**Read from a camera**, top right in the dialog, fills the list with the text overlays of one camera, so you can copy
them to many. An overlay placed freely on that camera keeps its place (**Custom position**).

All overlays go to view area 1. Image overlays and the other view areas stay as they are.

## What OADM checks first

- The camera supports text overlays.
- The text fits the camera's length, the size is in the camera's range and the view area has a free slot.

A refused camera fails with "Nothing was changed". An overlay the camera already shows is not added twice.

## Steps

Check the overlay support, Read the overlay limits, Read the overlays, Check the overlays, then one step per overlay
removed or added, Verify the overlays.

<Aside>
A line break in the text makes a new line on the video.
</Aside>
