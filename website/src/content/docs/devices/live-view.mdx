<Aside type="caution" title="Camera behind a port forward? Set its RTSP port">
OADM reaches the video and the events of a camera on port 554. Cameras behind a router that forwards another port, for
example a camera of the **AXIS Virtual Loan Tool**, need their port in OADM: right-click the camera in the device list,
**OADM** > **RTSP port**. See [RTSP port of a camera](#rtsp-port-of-a-camera).
</Aside>

<Image src={live} alt="Live view next to the device list, with zoom and focus over the picture" class="screen" />

- Click the device icon of a camera, encoder or intercom. The video opens on the right.
- Click another device's icon to switch, the same icon, the close button or Escape to close.
- Cameras with several sensors or view areas show buttons **1**, **2**, ... in the header to switch the source. OADM
  remembers the choice per device until you close the client.
- The header shows the codec, resolution and frame rate, or why there is no video.
- Drag the divider to give the video more room.

## Zoom and focus

Cameras with a remote zoom or focus lens show a bar over the bottom of the picture. Cameras without one show nothing.

- **Zoom** goes from wide to tele, **Focus** from near to far. The camera moves once you let go of the slider.
- **Autofocus** (focus icon) lets the camera focus by itself.
- **Autofocus area** (frame icon) shows the area the camera focuses on. Drag a new frame over the picture and click
  **Set**: the camera focuses there.
- **Reset focus** moves focus back to its default and focuses again.

<Image src={area} alt="Autofocus area drawn over the live picture" class="screen" />

You need the permission **Optics Control** (Operator has it). Every change is in the audit log.

## PTZ cameras

<Image src={ptz} alt="PTZ camera in the live view with the circle in the middle" class="screen" />

PTZ cameras show a circle in the middle of the picture and a bar with **Home**, the presets and a zoom slider.

- **Move**: drag from the circle. The further you drag, the faster the camera turns. Let go to stop.
- **Zoom**: the mouse wheel over the picture, or the zoom slider.
- **Center**: click a point of the picture and the camera turns there.
- **Presets**: choose one in the list and the camera goes there. **Home** goes to the home position.

You need the permission **Optics Control**. If the connection to the server breaks while you drag, the camera stops by
itself after a second.

## RTSP port of a camera

OADM gets the video and the events of a camera over RTSP, port 554. When a camera sits behind a router that forwards
another port to its port 554, tell OADM that port. Typical cases: cameras of the **AXIS Virtual Loan Tool**, a remote site
behind one public address, a lab with port forwards.

<Steps>

1. Right-click the camera in the device list. Several cameras at once work too.
2. Choose **OADM** > **RTSP port**.
3. Enter the forwarded port, for example `8554`, and click **Save**. **Use default** goes back to 554.

</Steps>

The port is stored in OADM only; nothing on the camera changes. The live view and the
[Metadata Monitor](../../tools/metadata-monitor/) use it. Changing it needs the permission **Optics Control**.

## Good to know

- The server opens the stream with the stored credentials. The client never gets the device password.
- Full HD at 25 fps, H.265 when the camera and client support it, else H.264.
- Several people watching the same camera share one connection to it.
- The video between camera and server is not encrypted.
- Devices with the status **Certificate changed** are not shown.
