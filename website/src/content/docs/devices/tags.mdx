Tags are your own labels, for example "Building A" or "PTZ". Each tag has a color.

<Steps>

1. Select devices, right-click and choose **Tags**.
2. Check the tags the devices should have. **New tag** creates one.
3. Click **OK**.

</Steps>

<Image src={dialog} alt="Tags dialog" class="screen" />

A check box shows a dash when only some of the selected devices have the tag. Click it once to give the tag to all.

## Group by tag

**Group by tag** in the toolbar shows one group per tag. A device with two tags appears in both groups. Devices
without a tag are in **No tag** at the end.

<Image src={grouped} alt="Device list grouped by tag" class="screen" />

## Good to know

- The search finds tags too.
- **Export** writes a Tags column. **Import from file** reads one (tags separated by `;`).
- Up to 20 tags per device, names up to 32 characters.
- Operators tag devices and create tags. Renaming, recoloring and deleting a tag needs an administrator.
