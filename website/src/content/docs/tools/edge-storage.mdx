Edge storage shows the SD card of the cameras you choose: its state, size, how full it is, how worn it is, whether it is encrypted
and how long recordings are kept. The tools of the camera's web interface work on many cameras at once.

<Image src={shot} alt="The Edge storage page with OK, worn and missing SD cards" class="screen" />

<Aside>
Edge storage is off by default. An administrator turns it on on the **Plugins** page.
</Aside>

## Check your SD cards

<Steps>

1. Open **Edge storage** in the navigation rail and choose the cameras with **Choose cameras**: all devices, tags, or
   single cameras. The page starts with the devices selected on the Devices page, else with your last choice.

   OADM reads the chosen cameras with an SD card slot once. The progress row counts the cameras.

2. Read the cards above the table:
   - **OK**: the card is in use and fine.
   - **Worn**: the card has used up as much of its life as the camera warns at (usually 90 %). Replace it soon.
   - **Problems**: the camera cannot use the card, for example it failed, is read-only or encrypted with another
     passphrase. The **SD card** column says why.
   - **No SD card**: the slot is empty.
   - **Not read**: OADM could not read the camera, for example because it rejects the stored login.
3. Click **Refresh** to read every card again.

</Steps>

## Change the SD cards

Select the cameras in the table, then choose a tool. Each camera gets its own task; the tasks pane below the table shows
the progress.

| Tool | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Check** | Checks the card for errors. Recording stops until the check is finished. |
| **Set retention** | How many days recordings are kept (1 to 7,000), or no age limit: then the camera keeps them until the card is full. |
| **Repair** | Repairs a damaged card. Damaged recordings can be lost. |
| **Encrypt** | Encrypts the card with a passphrase. The card is formatted: every recording on it is deleted. Keep the passphrase: the card cannot be read in another camera without it. |
| **Turn off encryption** | The card is formatted without encryption: every recording on it is deleted. |
| **Format** | Formats the card: every recording on it is deleted. |

OADM asks before every tool. Check, Repair, Format and encryption take the card out of use while they run and put it
back in use when they are done, also when something fails.

## Good to know

- OADM reads the cameras only when you open the page or click **Refresh**. Nothing runs in the background.
- Cameras with a changed certificate, rejected credentials or no password yet are not contacted.
- Operators may check cards and set the retention. Format, repair and encryption are for administrators unless a role
  allows them ("Format and encrypt storage" on the **Access** page).
- The passphrase is not stored by OADM.
- Network shares are not shown.
