A reader is one camera, one video source and up to 8 areas around the texts you want to read.

## Set up a reader

<Image src={settings} alt="The reader's settings: Name, Mode, Stream, Moving objects, the areas with their formats, a note and Save" class="screen fit" />

<Steps>

1. Click **New** and choose the camera. A saved reader keeps its camera: for another camera create another reader.
2. Enter a **Name** and choose the **Mode**.
3. Click the button under **Stream** to set the video source, resolution and frames per second.
4. [Draw an area](#areas) around each text you want to read.
5. Enter a [format](../formats/) for the area, or leave the field empty to read any text.
6. Check the table below the video, then click **Save**.

</Steps>

The table follows your changes before you save. These live results never start a rule: only saved readers do.

| Setting | What it does | New reader |
|---|---|---|
| **Name** | Shows in the event tree of the rules | `Reader 1`, `Reader 2` and so on |
| **Mode** | **Watching** or **On action** | **Watching** |
| **Video source** | The camera's view area | The camera's first |
| **Resolution** | Small text needs pixels: go higher before you draw larger areas | 1920x1080 or the largest below it |
| **Frames per second** | 1, 2, 4, 8, 12 or 15, only for **Watching** | 1 |
| **Moving objects** | For [belts and passing vehicles](../tuning/#belts-and-passing-vehicles), only for **Watching** | Off |

<Image src={stream} alt="The Stream settings dialog with Video source, Resolution and Frames per second" class="screen fit" />

## Watching or On action

| Mode | Reads | Sends **Text read** |
|---|---|---|
| **Watching** | All the time | When an area's text changes |
| **On action** | One picture each time a rule runs the action **Read text** | For every area, every time |

Start with 1 or 2 frames per second. A meter needs 1, a passing truck needs more. The top right of the page tells when the
server [no longer keeps up](../speed/#does-the-server-keep-up).

Renaming a reader or an area never stops the reading.

## Areas

Text is read only inside areas. A reader without an area reads nothing: the video is dimmed with **Start by drawing an
area**.

<Image src={drawing} alt="Drawing an area on the live video: a rectangle being dragged, the hint Drag to draw an area" class="screen" />

| To | Do this |
|---|---|
| Draw an area | **New** in the **Areas** header, then drag on the video. **Esc** stops |
| Select an area | Click it on the video or click its row. Click beside the areas to clear the selection |
| Move an area | Drag inside it, or use the arrow keys (**Shift** moves further) |
| Resize an area | Drag a handle. **Esc** cancels the drag |
| Rename or delete | The ⋯ menu of the area |
| Turn a read text into a format | **Use read text as format** in the ⋯ menu, see [Formats](../formats/#start-from-a-read-text) |

New areas are named **Area 1**, **Area 2** and so on, with the lowest free number. Rules see this name, so rename an area
to something like `Container number`. Areas stay in place when you change the resolution.

### Fixed place or Search

| Way | Size | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Fixed place | Up to about 960 pixels on the long side, tight around the text | A text that is always in one place: a meter, a sign, a number on a gate lane. Best and fastest |
| Search | Larger, up to the whole picture, with a format | A text that can be anywhere: an order number on a receipt, a label on a parcel |

The pixels are those of the reader's resolution: at 1920x1080 a Search starts at half the picture's width. A large area
with **Any text** reads everything in it, which rarely helps a rule.

### Several lines

An area can hold several lines. They are read top to bottom and left to right and joined with a space. A line that ends
with `-` is joined without one.

Draw one area per value. The number of lines found changes with light and focus, so "the second line" is not a stable
place.

## What it reads

| Text | Read |
|---|---|
| Latin script (English, German with umlauts, French and others) and digits | Yes |
| Up to 30 texts per picture, over all areas | Yes, the clearest first |
| Letters under about 10 pixels high | No: **Text too small** |
| Written top to bottom with one character per line, as on container doors | No |

## Camera angle

Text reads best when it faces the camera and stands upright.

| The text is | Reads well |
|---|---|
| Turned in the picture | Up to 10° either way, a container number up to 15° |
| Upside down | The same angles around upside down |
| Lying on its side | Only exactly on its side |
| A single line with **Any text** | At any angle |
| Tilted away from the camera | Up to 60°, from the side or from above |

Beyond that, mount the camera more frontal or zoom in. A turned value reads less surely when another line of text is close
to it.
