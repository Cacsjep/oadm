Live View shows several cameras at once, like the live view of a video management system. Save a view once and every
OADM user can open it.

<Image src={page} alt="Live View with the view Entrance open: three cameras in a grid" class="screen" />

<Aside>
Live View is off by default. An administrator turns it on on the **Plugins** page.
</Aside>

## Open a view

1. Open **Live View** in the navigation rail.
2. Click a view under **Views**. It opens in a tab; open more views and switch between the tabs.
3. Click a camera under **Cameras** to see it alone in its own tab.
4. Double-click a camera for fullscreen. Point at a camera for the same buttons as in the
   [live preview](../../devices/live-view/#over-the-picture) of the Devices page.

**Collapse** at the bottom of the list makes room for the video; the views stay as icons. Only the view you look at
streams; the cameras of other tabs pause and continue when you come back.

## Create or change a view

<Image src={edit} alt="A view in edit mode: grid lines, a frame around each camera and a camera dropped onto free space" class="screen" />

1. Click the New view button next to **Views**, or the pen next to a view. The bin next to it deletes the view.
2. Drag cameras from **Cameras** into the view. A frame shows where it will land: the largest free space where you drop
   it. A click on a camera puts it in the first free space.
3. Drag a camera to move it, drag one of its edges or corners to resize it, and click its X to remove it. Cameras snap
   to the grid and never overlap.
4. Enter a name and click **Save**. **Cancel** throws your changes away.

A view holds up to 16 cameras. On a camera with several view areas or sensors, the channel you choose while editing is
saved with the view.

## Good to know

- Views are stored on the OADM server and shared by every user. When someone else saves a view you have open, your tab
  follows.
- Creating, changing and deleting views needs the permission **Change views** on the
  [Permissions page](../../admin/users/#permissions-page). Viewers only open views. Seeing the video needs **Live view**.
- A camera that was removed from OADM keeps its place in the view and says so.
