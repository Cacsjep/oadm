The Image Health Dashboard shows what AXIS Image Health Analytics reports on each camera: blur, a blocked view, a
redirected camera, under-exposure and an unsuitable scene. One row per camera that has the app.

<Image src={shot} alt="The Image Health Dashboard with OK, pending and detected states" class="screen" />

<Aside>
The dashboard is off by default. An administrator turns it on on the **Plugins** page.
</Aside>

## Check your cameras

<Steps>

1. Open **Image Health Dashboard** in the navigation rail and choose the cameras with **Choose cameras**: all devices,
   tags, or single cameras. The page starts with the devices selected on the Devices page, else with your last choice.

   OADM asks the chosen cameras once. The progress row counts the cameras.

2. Read the cards above the table. Each camera with the app counts in one of Good, Detected, Pending and Errors, the
   worst state wins:
   - **Good**: the app runs and found nothing. A detection turned off in the app does not count against the camera.
   - **Detected**, **Pending**: the line below the count says which detections.
   - **Errors**: the app is not running, or OADM could not read the camera.
   - **Detection off**: cameras with at least one detection turned off, counted on top of the other cards.
3. Read the table. Each detection shows:
   - **OK**: nothing found.
   - **Pending**: the app saw something and waits to confirm it.
   - **Detected**: the app confirmed it.
   - **Off**: the detection is turned off in the app.
4. Click **Refresh** to ask the cameras again, or turn on **Auto refresh** to ask every 10 seconds while the page is
   open.

</Steps>

The **App** column shows **Running**, **Not running** (the app is installed but stopped), or why OADM could not read
the camera. **Last change** shows when a detection changed between two checks. Cameras without the app are not listed;
the line above the table counts them. The cards always count every camera, also while you search.

## Good to know

- Nothing runs in the background, and nothing runs after you leave the page.
- To react to blur and the other detections in [event rules](../event-rules/), create a
  [device trigger](../device-triggers/) on an event of the app, for example **Image Health Analytics - Blur** or
  **Image Health Analytics - Any-Active-Events**.
- OADM asks 24 cameras at a time and waits at most 3 seconds for each.
- The dashboard only reads. It never changes a camera or the app settings.
- Devices that need a login, have no password yet, have a changed certificate or do not answer show the reason.

<Aside>
Operators can use the dashboard. No administrator rights are needed.
</Aside>
