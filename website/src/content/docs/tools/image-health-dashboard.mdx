The Image Health Dashboard shows what AXIS Image Health Analytics reports on each camera: blur, a blocked view, a
redirected camera, under-exposure and an unsuitable scene. One row per camera that has the app.

<Image src={shot} alt="The Image Health Dashboard with OK, pending and detected states" class="screen" />

<Aside>
The dashboard is off by default. An administrator turns it on on the **Plugins** page.
</Aside>

## Check your cameras

<Steps>

1. Open **Image Health Dashboard** in the navigation rail.

   OADM asks every camera once. The progress row counts the cameras.

2. Read the table. Each detection shows:
   - **OK**: nothing found.
   - **Pending**: the app saw something and waits to confirm it.
   - **Detected**: the app confirmed it.
   - **Off**: the detection is turned off in the app.
3. Click **Refresh** to ask every camera again, or turn on **Auto refresh** to ask every 10 seconds while the page is
   open.

</Steps>

The **App** column shows **Running**, **Not running** (the app is installed but stopped), or why OADM could not read
the camera. **Last change** shows when a detection changed between two checks. The line above the table counts the
cameras with the app running, with a detection, pending, not running, without the app and those that could not be
checked.

## Good to know

- OADM asks the cameras only when you open the page, click **Refresh** or turn on **Auto refresh**. Nothing runs in
  the background, and nothing runs after you leave the page.
- Cameras without the app are counted but not listed.
- OADM asks 24 cameras at a time and waits at most 3 seconds for each.
- The dashboard only reads. It never changes a camera or the app settings.
- Devices that need a login, have no password yet, have a changed certificate or do not answer show the reason.

<Aside>
Operators can use the dashboard. No administrator rights are needed.
</Aside>
