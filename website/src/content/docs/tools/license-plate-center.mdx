AXIS License Plate Verifier (ALPV) reads plates on the camera. Each camera keeps its own lists. License Plate Center keeps
**one plate list** for all of your cameras. **Tags** decide on which cameras a plate is allowed. OADM decides on every
read whether the plate is allowed there, shows every read in a live log and sends events to your
[event rules](../event-rules/), for example to open a barrier.

License Plate Center is off by default. An administrator turns it on on the **Plugins** page. It is in the navigation
rail under Integrations.

<Aside>
License Plate Center works with ALPV 3 only. ALPV 3.1.7 needs AXIS OS 12.11.77 or newer. Install ALPV and its license
with the [Applications](../../tasks/applications/) tasks.
</Aside>

## How it works

- A plate is in the list once, with its tags, for example **Garage Nord** and **Staff**.
- Each camera has its **allowed tags**. A plate is allowed on a camera when it has one of them. A camera without allowed
  tags allows no plate.
- **Allowed from** and **Allowed to** limit a plate to a time, for example a visitor for three days.
- OADM connects to every camera itself. Nothing is set up on the camera for it, and the server needs no open port.
- The cameras' own lists (Allow, Block, Custom) and ALPV's access control (relay, I/O, door controller) are never read
  or changed.

Every read gets one result:

| Result | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Allowed** | The plate is in the list and allowed on this camera now. |
| **Not allowed** | The plate is in the list, but not on this camera, not yet or no longer. |
| **Unknown plate** | The plate is not in the list. |

A plate the camera reads exactly as in the list is decided at once. Other reads wait up to 3 seconds for the camera's
best read of that car, so a misread at the first sight does not count.

## Get started

<Steps>

1. On the **Cameras** tab, click **Add cameras** and choose the cameras with ALPV.
2. Select a camera and click **Set up**: draw the areas, check the region and the camera location, choose the
   **Allowed tags** and click **Save**.
3. An administrator chooses the folder for the log in **Settings**. Without a folder, nothing is kept, but the events
   still fire.
4. On the **Plates** tab, add your plates with their tags, or **Import** a list.
5. Watch the reads arrive on the **Log** tab.

</Steps>

## Log

<Image src={log} alt="The Log tab with reads, plate pictures, results and confidence" class="screen" />

Every read, newest first and live. **Pause** stops the list from moving; reads are still kept.

- **Plate** is the plate of the list, or what the camera read when no plate matched. A warning triangle means the camera
  read it with a small difference; the tooltip shows what it read.
- **Result** is an icon: green check Allowed, red Not allowed, grey question mark Unknown plate. The tooltip says why,
  for example "Not allowed, ended 31 Oct 2026".
- **Confidence** is how sure the camera was, 0 to 100. Below 75 it shows in orange, below 60 in red.
- A row "Connection lost from 13:58 to 14:06" shows when OADM could not hear a camera. Reads of that time are lost.

**Filter** adds a filter: time, result, tags, area, direction, country or vehicle (type, colour, make, model). Each
filter shows as a chip below the toolbar. Click a chip to change it, its x to remove it. The search finds a plate from
its start or a name of the list.

**Choose columns** (the icon left of the search) shows more columns, such as Read as, Country or Vehicle make. Drag a
header to move a column. OADM remembers your columns on this computer.

**Add to plates** adds the selected unknown plate to the list. **Export** saves what the log shows, at most the newest
10,000 reads, as **CSV** or **PDF** with the columns you chose. The PDF has the plate pictures when it has at most 2,000
reads and 11 other columns.

## Plates

<Image src={plates} alt="The Plates tab with plates, names, tags and dates" class="screen" />

- **Add plate**: the plate, a name, tags, **Allowed from** and **Allowed to** (both optional). With **Delete when it
  ends**, OADM deletes the plate after its Allowed to. Without it, the plate stays and is Not allowed from then on.
- A plate is stored in capitals, without spaces, dashes or dots: "W-123 45 A" becomes W12345A.
- **Change tags** adds or removes tags of the selected plates. **Manage tags** renames tags and changes their colours.
- **Delete** deletes the selected plates.

### Company cars

<Image src={wildcard} alt="The Add plate dialog with the plate AXIS12** and the hint below it" class="screen" />

One entry for many cars: `*` stands for exactly one letter or digit. `AXIS12**` allows AXIS1234 and AXIS12AB, not
AXIS123 or AXIS12345. Combine it with Allowed to for a fleet that ends at a date.

- A wildcard plate needs at least 3 letters or digits besides `*`. At most 1,000 wildcard plates.
- A plate entered on its own always decides first. If AXIS1234 ended yesterday, it is Not allowed, even though AXIS12**
  would allow it.
- The Log shows what the camera read (AXIS1234); the tooltip of the plate says "Matches AXIS12**".

### Import and export

<Image src={importPreview} alt="The import preview with new, changed and refused rows" class="screen" />

**Import** takes a CSV file: ALPV's own export (plate, date, description; the description becomes the name) or OADM's
export. Before anything is saved, the dialog shows the new, changed and refused plates. Choose **Tags for every plate**
to tag all imported plates at once. **Export** saves the list as CSV.

## Cameras

<Image src={cameras} alt="The Cameras tab with status, allowed tags and image settings" class="screen" />

- **Name** is the camera's name in License Plate Center. The Log, the statistics and the events use it. Change it in Set
  up. The device keeps its own name in OADM.
- **Status** says whether OADM hears the camera, or what is wrong: Events are off in ALPV, ALPV is stopped, ALPV is not
  installed, ALPV has no license, ALPV 3 is needed, ALPV reads another sensor, Site offline.
- **Image settings** shows whether the camera's image settings are the ones ALPV recommends for plates. The tooltip lists
  each value next to the recommended one. **Use recommended image settings** applies them on the selected cameras, one
  task per camera.
- **Allowed tags** sets the tags of the selected cameras. **Copy settings** copies the recognition settings of one camera
  to others, the areas only when you choose so, one task per camera.
- **Remove** takes the cameras out of License Plate Center. Nothing on the cameras changes.

### Set up a camera

<Image src={setup} alt="The Set up dialog with two areas on the live picture and the recognition settings" class="screen" />

- **Areas**: drag the corners on the live picture. Double-click an edge to add a corner, a corner to remove it (3 to 8
  corners). Turn the arrow to the driving direction that counts as **In**. **Add area 2** for a second lane.
- **Recognition**: Region, Camera location, the plate width in pixels, the number of characters and the **Confidence
  threshold**. Only the settings the camera has are shown.
- **Allowed tags**: the plates allowed on this camera.

**Save** writes the changes to the camera right away and checks that the camera kept them. Only what you changed is
written. Save also turns on the events OADM needs in ALPV.

ALPV reads the first sensor of a camera, and License Plate Center works with that sensor only. For barrier, relay,
storage and ALPV's own lists, use ALPV's own page on the camera.

## Stats

<Image src={stats} alt="The Stats tab with read counts, confidence per camera and charts over time" class="screen" />

The reads of the last hour, 24 hours, 7 days and in total; the average plate and vehicle confidence per camera; reads and
confidence over time, in total and per camera. Click a camera in the legend to show or hide its line. A camera with a
low confidence often needs other image settings, a better angle or another plate width.

## Events for your rules

| Event | When |
| --- | --- |
| **Plate allowed** | A read was Allowed. |
| **Plate not allowed** | A read was Not allowed. |
| **Unknown plate** | A read was an Unknown plate. |
| **Plate read** | Every read, with its result. |
| **Camera problem** | A camera has been lost for 30 seconds, ALPV stopped or its events are off. |
| **Any camera problem** | Any camera has a problem, or the log cannot be kept. |

The read events carry the plate, what the camera read, the name, the tags, the direction, the area, the country, the
confidence and the vehicle's type and colour. To open a gate, make a rule on **Plate allowed** for that camera with the
action that switches its output.

## Settings

<Image src={settings} alt="The settings dialog of License Plate Center" class="screen" />

Administrators only.

- **Allowed character deviation**: Exact, 1 or 2 characters (default 1). A wrong, a missing or an extra character each
  count as 1. A near match is only made with plates allowed on that camera.
- **Wait for the best read**: 1 to 10 seconds (default 3).
- **Keep reads for** (days) and **Maximum size** (GB): older reads go first.
- **Folder on the server**: where the log is kept. **Choose folder** browses the server's folders. The log is not part
  of a backup.
- **Keep plate pictures** and **Keep unknown plates in the log**.

## Good to know

- Plates are personal data. Choose the days and size of the log to fit your rules, and give the log permissions only to
  those who need them.
- Reads while OADM cannot reach a camera are lost; the camera does not keep them for OADM.
- A camera with a rotated picture is set up on ALPV's own page.
- License Plate Center is no parking management: no tickets, no payment and no occupancy count.

<Aside>
Permissions: **View License Plate Center**, **View plate log**, **Export plate log**, **Manage plates** and **Set up
cameras**. Users limited to a device group see only the cameras and reads of their group. The plate list is the same for
everyone.
</Aside>
