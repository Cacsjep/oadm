The I/O Monitor shows the inputs and outputs of one device and updates them several times a second. Use it to check
wiring: close a door contact or switch a relay and watch its contact close.

<Image src={shot} alt="The I/O Monitor with inputs and outputs of an I/O module" class="screen" />

<Aside>
The I/O Monitor is off by default. An administrator turns it on on the **Plugins** page.
</Aside>

## Use it

1. Open **I/O Monitor** in the navigation rail.
2. Click **Choose a device** and pick the device. The list shows every device with inputs or outputs; type in the
   search to find it.
3. The device is drawn as a row of inputs and a row of outputs. Each port shows its contact, drawn open or closed;
   green means **Active**. Point at a port to see its normal state.

The port number under each contact is the number to use in the **Set output** action of [event rules](../event-rules/).

## Good to know

- OADM reads the device only while the page shows it. Choosing another device or leaving the page stops it.
- If the connection to the server drops or the server restarts, the page starts reading again on its own.
- The I/O Monitor only reads. It never switches an output.
- Devices that need a login, have no password yet or have a changed certificate show the reason instead.
