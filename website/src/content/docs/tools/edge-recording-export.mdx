Edge Recording Export downloads the recordings that cameras keep on their SD card or network share, from many cameras at
once, into a folder on this computer.

<Image src={page} alt="Edge Recording Export after a search: five cameras with their recordings, sizes and status" class="screen" />

## Find recordings

1. Edge Recording Export is off when OADM is installed: an administrator turns it on under **Plugins**.
2. Open **Edge Recording Export** in the navigation rail.
3. Click **Choose cameras** and pick cameras or tags. The button then shows what is chosen. Only devices with video are listed.
4. Choose **All recordings**, or **Time range** with **From** and **To** (for example `2026-10-12 08:00`). The times are
   this computer's time.
5. Click **Search**. Each camera shows how many recordings it has in that time, about how large they are, and the first
   and last recording.

A recording that only partly falls into the time range is cut to it.

When the card is the problem, the camera's row says so, for example **No SD card or network share**, **The SD card
failed** or **The SD card is read-only**. A full card is normal: the camera removes the oldest recordings.

## Download

<Image src={download} alt="A download in progress: the progress bar, the current recording and Stop" class="screen" />

1. Click **Download** and choose a folder.
2. OADM downloads one recording after the other. Each camera gets its own folder, for example
   `10.0.0.48 P3265-V B8A44F631339`, and each file keeps the name the camera gives it.
3. **Stop** ends the download. The finished files stay; the next **Download** into the same folder skips them and
   continues.

The download keeps running while you work on other pages. It ends when you log out.

## Good to know

- Files are MP4 when the camera offers it, otherwise Matroska (`.mkv`). Both play in VLC; MP4 also plays in the players
  of Windows and macOS.
- The recordings travel from the camera through the OADM server to this computer. The camera's password never reaches
  this computer, and nothing is stored on the server.
- Who may download is the right **Export recordings** on the Users page (Operator has it, Viewer does not). Every
  downloaded recording is in the audit log.
- Cameras that need a login, have no password yet or show **Certificate changed** are not asked.
